    Chee-En Yu¶

    M.S. student at National Taiwan University working on large audio language models, speech generation, and machine learning — interested in test-time adaptation, reasoning, and RL for speech.

    About me¶

    I'm currently a graduate student in Electrical Engineering at National Taiwan University, working in the Speech Processing and Machine Learning (SPML) Lab under Prof. Hung-Yi Lee. My research centers on large audio language models, Text-to-Speech, and bringing reasoning and reinforcement learning into speech systems.

    I'm passionate about machine learning, speech processing, and real-world applications of deep learning. Feel free to connect! 🚀

⚙️ ReasonTTS: Reasoning-Aligned Instructional Speech Generation (2025 - Present)

A multi-hop reasoning framework that lets the TTS model self-refine its generated speech through iterative reasoning, with reinforcement learning integrated into the generation pipeline to improve quality and instruction-following.

😄 HAHAHA: Humor-Aligned and Human-Aligned Hilarious Agent (2025 - Present)

Uses real-world social-media engagement signals (e.g. "likes") as implicit human feedback to build a reward signal for humor generation, training a ranking-based reward model and applying PPO to align toward human-perceived funniness.
